✳THE MEANING OF MOLI

A NAME INSPIRED BY JASMINE

Grace in every detail.
Character in every flavour.

MOLI takes its name from the Chinese word for the white jasmine flower, a symbol of purity and grace. Inspired by the richness of Chinese culinary heritage, we bring familiar flavours into a new light with thoughtful craft, beautiful presentation and a contemporary spirit.
